# AeroSkills course content standard

Each module must function as a genuine learning unit rather than a short article.

## Minimum structure

- Five lessons per module.
- Approximately 25–35 minutes of learning per module.
- Clear learning objectives.
- Technical explanation with aviation context.
- Realistic engineering, operational or regulatory trade-offs.
- One practical aviation case in every lesson.
- One reflection or decision prompt in every lesson.
- A concise key takeaway.
- Official sources and further reading.
- Five-question assessment with explanatory feedback.

## Editorial principles

- Use plain international English without oversimplifying the technical content.
- Separate measured evidence, regulatory requirements, industry ambition and future scenarios.
- State uncertainty and system boundaries.
- Avoid presenting public corporate targets as guaranteed outcomes.
- Avoid confidential or internal Airbus information.
- Prefer primary sources such as Airbus public documentation, EASA, ICAO, the European Commission, IPCC and recognised aviation standards bodies.
